# Research roadmap

This roadmap identifies work to investigate; it is not a claim that sources have been reviewed or data acquired.

1. Define target species and municipal decision questions with public-health and infrastructure stakeholders.
2. Build evidence registry entries from verifiable peer-reviewed literature and official documents. Record DOI/URL, location, species, method, findings, Taipei applicability, limitations and confidence.
3. Identify observation processes and bias: reporting access, survey coverage, detection probability, time, location precision and missingness.
4. Inventory candidate city datasets with license, update cycle, geographic coverage, CRS, access terms and provenance before download.
5. Extract biological/environmental parameters only with source evidence and units; unknown stays unknown. Distinguish correlation from causal mechanism.
6. Have independent reviewer challenge evidence transferability to Taipei before model assumptions are accepted.

Each study follows Evidence → Assumptions → Model → Validation. Do not describe a literature association as a causal intervention effect without causal evidence.
