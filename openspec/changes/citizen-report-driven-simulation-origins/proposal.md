# Citizen-report-driven simulation origins

## Why

Project-owner attestation authorizes continued assessment and attribution of Rat Radar citizen-report data, while written publisher terms and scientific review remain distinct evidence requirements. The workflow needs a privacy-preserving aggregate-cell contract and deterministic synthetic-fixture resolver without converting reports into animal observations or biological weights.

## What Changes

- Add an additive `origin_cell_id` request contract with explicit provenance and safe rejection states.
- Add deterministic in-cell resolution over synthetic fixtures only; no centroid or distant nearest-node snapping.
- Render published aggregate cells as representative red markers with a click-to-inspect detail card; focusing a marker does not run a simulation, while an explicit card action may use that cell as the origin.
- Preserve source attribution: 資料來源：見鼠雷達 Rat Radar — https://ratdar.taipei/
- Keep real-source execution and redistribution blocked until applicable terms and scientific approvals are evidenced.

## Scope boundary

Citizen-report counts remain report counts. They do not imply animal counts, prevalence, movement, risk, habitat, or traversability. Existing synthetic/node demonstration remains available as an isolated development path.
