# Adversarial review

Initial risks retained for implementation:

- Synthetic numbers and paths can be mistaken for observed activity, risk, or migration.
- Progressive disclosure must not hide classification, uncertainty, provenance, or unavailable state.
- Reports and trajectories must remain separate with no join or validation implication.
- Date conversion must remain Asia/Taipei and inclusive; machine timezone must not affect results.
- Tiny labels and mobile stacking may hide the highest-risk scientific qualifiers.
- Accessibility must cover map alternatives, disabled/unavailable controls, focus, live errors, contrast, zoom, and reduced motion.
- OSM provenance/hash evidence must remain internally consistent; bounded Songshan coverage must be visible.

Impeccable `buildPath` was unavailable in this repository context; source-level detector and rendered URL detector were used instead, with the gap recorded here rather than silently inferred.

Decision: implementation may proceed within the documented frontend-only scope. Final gate remains conditional until browser, accessibility, Municipal Analyst, and reviewer evidence is recorded.
