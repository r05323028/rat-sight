## Intent

Connect RatSight's existing bounded surface-network simulation, synthetic report-shaped fixtures, provenance UI, and topology diagnostics into a future evidence-informed workflow without misrepresenting reports, proxies, or simulated paths as biological evidence.

## Current decision

This change is **conditionally blocked for full P0 implementation**. Current repository evidence supports only synthetic reports, bounded Songshan OSM surface geometry, and stormwater reference data. Rat Radar reuse is unauthorized/unverified; food-business, market, and sanitary-sewer contracts are not verified. No raw real records may be downloaded or ingested.

Safe engineering slice implemented separately in the active simulation change: explicit origin, step playback, reachability disclosure, termination reasons, and scenario/environment honesty copy.

## Scope when approvals exist

- Verified source contracts and local-file importers for authorized observations and environmental references.
- Report-informed candidate-origin distributions with privacy and bias controls.
- Explicit, evidence-reviewed environment-aware transition weights.
- Comparable baseline/report-origin/environment scenarios and explanations.

## Non-goals

No population model, risk score, migration claim, automatic sewer connectivity, business accusation, live scraping, ML, or unapproved biological constants.
