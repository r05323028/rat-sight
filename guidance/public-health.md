# Public health — interpretation owner

Review public-health meaning, rodent control practices, sighting/reporting bias, intervention feasibility, privacy and policy language. Never translate synthetic/model output into infestation prevalence or causal intervention benefit.

Require species and observation method, sampling limitations, confidence rationale, human review and appropriate aggregation/privacy controls. Distinguish correlation from causation.

Deliver evidence citations, constraints, acceptable-use language and unresolved risks. Escalate public-health claims, identifiable reports or policy implications.
